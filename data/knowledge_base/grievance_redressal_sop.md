# Public Grievance Redressal Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) 2026

## 1. Purpose & Guiding Principles
The Central Civic Grievance Cell operates under the Department of Public Administration to ensure transparent, accountable, and time-bound resolution of citizen complaints.

## 2. Grievance Categories & Allocation
- **Water Supply & Drainage Issues:** Routed to Urban Water Supply Department (UWSD). Target resolution: 48 hours for leaks, 7 days for pipeline repairs.
- **Streetlight & Electric Hazards:** Routed to Municipal Electrical Division. Target resolution: 24 to 48 hours.
- **Garbage & Solid Waste Management:** Routed to Sanitation & Health Department. Target resolution: 24 hours.
- **Road Potholes & Civil Maintenance:** Routed to Public Works Department (PWD). Target resolution: 14 days.
- **Property Tax & Assessment Disputes:** Routed to Municipal Revenue Department (REV). Target resolution: 15 business days.

## 3. Citizen Rights & Escalation Workflow
1. **Filing:** Any citizen can lodge a grievance online specifying the department, subject, and detailed description.
2. **Acknowledgment:** A unique Grievance Tracking ID is generated immediately.
3. **Officer Review:** The assigned Department Officer reviews the problem and posts official resolution notes.
4. **Appeals:** If dissatisfied with the resolution, the citizen may request higher administrative review within 30 days of closure.

## 4. Grounding & Anti-Guarantee Disclaimer
Grievance submission ensures administrative review and field inspection; it does not guarantee monetary compensation, waiver of statutory penalties, or legal indemnity.
