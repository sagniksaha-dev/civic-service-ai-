# Municipal Water Supply Connection Guidelines 2026

## 1. Objective and Scope
This policy outlines the official procedure for domestic and commercial water pipeline connections under the Urban Water Supply Department (UWSD).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
1. The applicant must be at least 18 years of age.
2. The applicant must hold legal ownership or authorized tenancy of the property situated within municipal limits.
3. There must be no outstanding municipal tax arrears on the premises.

## 3. Mandatory Documents Required
Applicants must submit copies of the following non-sensitive records:
- Proof of Address (e.g. Registered Rental Agreement, Electricity Bill, or Municipal Assessment Notice).
- Property Holding Tax Receipt of the current financial year.
- Building Layout Plan indicating plumbing hookup points.
- Completed Standard Civic Application Form.

Note: No Aadhaar or national identity cards are stored or required.

## 4. Applicable Processing Fees and Security Deposits
- Domestic Connection Application Fee: Rs. 500 (Non-refundable).
- Water Meter Security Deposit: Rs. 1,500 (Refundable upon disconnection).
- Road Cutting Restoration Charge: Variable based on engineering survey.

## 5. Standard Processing Time and Workflow
1. Application Submission: Submitted online with generated reference number (APP-YYYYMMDD-XXXX).
2. Document Scrutiny: Completed within 3 working days by the Department Officer.
3. Site Inspection: Field engineer inspects pipeline feasibility within 5 working days.
4. Final Approval & Meter Installation: Completed within 7 to 10 working days from fee verification. Total standard processing time is 15 business days.

## 6. Grievance and Help
For complaints regarding pipeline leakage or low pressure, citizens may file a grievance referencing their Department code UWSD.
