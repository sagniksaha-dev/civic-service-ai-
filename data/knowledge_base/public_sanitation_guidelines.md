# Municipal Solid Waste & Public Sanitation Guidelines 2026

## 1. Overview
The Department of Public Sanitation (`DPS-SAN`) is responsible for city-wide solid waste management, door-to-door segregated garbage collection, septic tank cleaning, and commercial bulk waste clearance.

## 2. Bulk Commercial Waste Collection & Sanitation Clearance (`SAN-BULK-01`)
Commercial complexes, banquet halls, hospitals, and market associations producing over 50 kg of waste per day are classified as Bulk Waste Generators.

### Submission Requirements:
1. Trade License or Municipal Property Tax receipt.
2. Segregated on-site waste storage facility declaration (Wet / Dry / Hazardous).
3. Dedicated composting or bio-waste tie-up agreement.

### Fees & Timelines:
- Processing Fee: **Rs. 800 per month or Rs. 5,000 annual subscription**
- SLA Timeline: **7 business days for service activation**

## 3. Public Sanitation Grievances
Citizens can file complaints regarding overflowing dumpsters, uncollected garbage, or blocked open drains. The standard turnaround time for drain unclogging and waste clearance is **24 hours**.
