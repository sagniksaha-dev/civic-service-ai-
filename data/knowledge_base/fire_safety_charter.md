# Municipal Fire Safety & Disaster Management Charter 2026

## 1. Overview & Jurisdiction
The Fire Safety & Disaster Management Authority (`FIRE-EMERG`) oversees fire hazard mitigation, emergency preparedness, structural fire audits, and mandatory Fire Safety No Objection Certificate (NOC) issuances across all commercial, industrial, high-rise residential, and institutional buildings within the municipal jurisdiction.

## 2. Mandatory Fire Safety NOC Clearance (`FIRE-NOC-01`)
All commercial enterprises, hotels, restaurants, educational buildings, hospitals, and residential complexes exceeding 15 meters in height are mandated to obtain an official Fire Safety NOC prior to commencement of operations or building occupancy.

### Required Documentation:
1. Approved architectural floor plans depicting designated emergency fire exits, staircases, and assembly areas.
2. Proof of installation of certified firefighting systems:
   - ISI-marked ABC Dry Powder & CO2 Fire Extinguishers on every floor.
   - Overhead dedicated fire water storage tank (minimum 25,000 liters capacity for high-rises).
   - Functional automatic sprinkler systems and smoke detectors in common corridors.
3. Structural stability and electrical load safety certificate issued by a licensed electrical/structural engineer.
4. Proof of ownership or registered commercial lease deed.

### Fee Structure & SLA Timelines:
- Standard Commercial Premises (< 2,000 sq ft): **Rs. 2,500**
- Large Commercial / High-Rise Buildings (> 2,000 sq ft or > 15m height): **Rs. 5,000**
- Preliminary Scrutiny & Document Verification: **3 business days**
- Mandatory Physical On-Site Fire Inspection: **7 business days**
- Final Fire Safety NOC Issuance: **15 business days (Statutory SLA)**

## 3. Annual Fire Safety Renewal & Audit (`FIRE-AUDIT-01`)
Fire Safety NOC certificates are valid for a duration of **1 year (12 months)** from the date of issuance.
- Annual Renewal Fee: **Rs. 1,000**
- Mandatory Submission: Annual fire extinguisher refilling certificates and bi-annual fire drill evacuation logs.
- Renewal Processing SLA: **7 business days**

## 4. Disaster Management & Emergency Rapid Response
In the event of localized urban flooding, structural collapse, or chemical fire hazards:
- Emergency 24/7 Disaster Helpline: Dial **101** or Municipal Emergency Hotline **1800-CIVIC-FIRE**.
- Post-Disaster Relief Grievances: Redressed within **24 to 48 hours** under emergency escalation protocol.
