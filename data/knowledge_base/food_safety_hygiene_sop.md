# Municipal Public Health & Food Safety Inspection SOP 2026

## 1. Overview
The Department of Public Health & Food Safety (`DPH-FOOD`) regulates food hygiene standards, mosquito vector control, municipal immunization, and commercial food establishment certifications within municipal borders.

## 2. Commercial Food Hygiene & Safety Rating Certificate (`FOOD-HYG-01`)
All restaurants, cafes, bakeries, food processing units, and street vendor clusters must acquire an official Food Hygiene & Safety Rating Certificate.

### Mandatory Requirements:
1. Valid FSSAI License or Registration Copy.
2. Medical fitness certificates of all food handlers and kitchen staff.
3. Pest control treatment contract records (quarterly mandatory).
4. Potable water testing lab report (microbial and chemical safety).
5. Commercial premises lease deed or property tax receipt.

### Fee Structure and Timelines:
- Small Eateries & Bakeries: **Rs. 1,500**
- Large Commercial Restaurants & Hotels: **Rs. 3,500**
- Processing & Inspection SLA: **10 business days**

## 3. Public Health Drives & Disease Vector Mitigation
The department conducts seasonal vector-control fogging, larvicidal spraying, and municipal immunization drives across all wards. Citizens can report stagnant water or mosquito breeding via the Grievance Redressal portal with a 48-hour resolution SLA.
