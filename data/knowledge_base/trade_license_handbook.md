# Commercial Trade License Issuance Handbook

## 1. Regulatory Framework
All commercial establishments, retail shops, manufacturing units, and food businesses operating within municipal borders must obtain a valid Trade License from the Commerce & Licensing Department.

## 2. Requirements and Mandatory Clearances
- Proof of Commercial Property Ownership or Registered Rent Agreement.
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Fire & Emergency Services Department (mandatory for premises exceeding 500 sq ft or eateries).
- Health & Sanitation Trade Clearance Certificate (for food and beverage outlets).
- Building Occupancy Certificate.

## 3. Validity and Annual Renewal
- Trade licenses are valid for one financial year (April 1 to March 31).
- Renewal applications must be submitted by February 28 of the prevailing year.
- Failure to renew results in a 25% administrative surcharge.

## 4. Disclaimers and Inspection
The issuance of a trade license is contingent upon on-site physical inspection by municipal health and safety officers. All approvals are subject to official verification.
