# Municipal Transport & Traffic Management Permits 2026

## 1. Overview
The Municipal Transport & Traffic Management Authority (`MUNI-TRANS`) regulates road cutting permissions, commercial street parking allocations, electric vehicle (EV) charging zone setups, and intelligent street lighting infrastructure.

## 2. Road Digging NOC & Utility Trenching Permit (`TRANS-ROAD-01`)
Telecom providers, pipeline agencies, and private developers requiring road excavation for utility laying must obtain an official Road Digging NOC.

### Requirements:
1. Approved utility trenching route map.
2. Security deposit for road surface restoration.
3. Traffic diversion plan approved by Traffic Police.

### Fees & Timelines:
- Restoration Deposit: **Rs. 3,000 per linear meter** (Refundable post-restoration verification).
- SLA Timeline: **14 business days**
