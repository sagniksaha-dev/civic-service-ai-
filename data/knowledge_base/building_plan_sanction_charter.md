# Municipal Building Plan Sanction & Permit Charter 2026

## 1. Overview
Governs architectural approvals for residential and commercial building construction within the Municipal Corporation jurisdiction under the Department of Urban Planning & Infrastructure.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements
1. The applicant must be the registered property owner or legal power of attorney holder.
2. The proposed plot must have clear title with up-to-date property tax payment receipts.
3. Building drawings must be prepared and signed by an accredited Licensed Building Surveyor (LBS) or Registered Architect.

## 3. Required Submission Documents
- Architectural Floor Plans, Elevation, and Section Drawings (AutoCAD/PDF format).
- Structural Stability Certificate from an empanelled Structural Engineer.
- Site Ownership Title Deed / Mutation Certificate.
- Fire Safety Clearance NOC (for buildings exceeding 15 meters in height).
- Environmental Pollution Control Board NOC (for commercial complexes).

## 4. Processing Fees and Timelines
- Residential Building Plan Sanction Fee: Rs. 25 per square meter of built-up area.
- Commercial Plan Sanction Fee: Rs. 60 per square meter of built-up area.
- Preliminary Scrutiny: 5 business days.
- Site Inspection: 10 business days.
- Final Sanction Issuance: 30 business days (SLA Target).

## 5. Violation Notice
Unauthorized constructions departing from the sanctioned plan are subject to immediate stop-work notices and demolition under Section 400 of the Municipal Corporation Act.
