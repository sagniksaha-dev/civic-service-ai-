MUNICIPAL PROPERTY TAX ASSESSMENT & REBATE POLICY

Section 1: General Information
Property tax is levied annually on all residential and commercial properties within the municipal jurisdiction by the Revenue and Property Administration Department.

Section 2: Calculation & Due Dates
- Property tax is assessed based on the built-up area, property classification, and annual rateable value.
- Annual tax returns must be filed on or before June 30th of each financial year.
- Early Payment Rebate: A 10% rebate is granted if full payment is cleared before April 30th.
- Late Payment Surcharge: A penalty of 1.5% per month is applicable on outstanding dues after June 30th.

Section 3: Required Supporting Documents for Self-Assessment
1. Previous Year Property Tax Receipt.
2. Building Sanction Plan or Completion Certificate.
3. Utility Connection Bill (Electricity or Water) for address verification.
4. Title Deed or Registered Conveyance Deed copy.

Section 4: Grievance and Correction Process
Citizens disputing the property tax assessment or square footage calculation may submit a formal grievance under the Revenue Department with their property reference number. Corrections are addressed within 14 working days.
