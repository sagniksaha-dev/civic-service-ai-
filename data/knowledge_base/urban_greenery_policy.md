# Department of Environment & Urban Greenery Policy 2026

## 1. Overview
The Department of Environment & Urban Greenery (`ENV-PARKS`) manages public municipal parks, botanical reserves, urban afforestation drives, ambient air quality monitors, and dangerous tree pruning operations.

## 2. Dangerous Tree Trimming & Arboriculture Clearance (`ENV-TREE-01`)
Citizens and housing societies must obtain municipal permission before trimming overgrown trees or felling dead/hazardous trees threatening power lines or structural integrity.

### Requirements:
1. Proof of premise ownership or municipal ward councillor recommendation.
2. Photographs showing the hazardous branch or tree obstruction.
3. Site inspection access approval.

### Fees & Timelines:
- Inspection Fee: **Rs. 300**
- SLA Timeline: **5 business days** (Emergency branch clearance within **12 hours**).
