# Civil Registration: Birth & Death Certificates Policy 2026

## 1. Scope & Objective
This guideline governs the issuance of official Birth and Death Certificates under the Department of Vital Statistics & Civil Registration.

## 2. Eligibility & Application Period
- **Birth Registration:** Must be reported within 21 days of occurrence without penalty. Delayed registration up to 1 year requires an affidavit and approval of the Sub-Divisional Magistrate.
- **Death Registration:** Must be reported within 21 days of occurrence by immediate family members or hospital authority.
- The applicant must be a resident of the municipality or the event must have occurred within municipal jurisdiction.

## 3. Required Documents
- For Birth Registration:
  - Hospital Discharge Certificate or Institutional Birth Report.
  - Parents' Marriage Certificate or joint address verification.
  - Standard Municipal Application Form.
- For Death Registration:
  - Hospital Medical Cause of Death Certificate or Crematorium/Burial report.
  - Proof of relation of applicant to the deceased.

## 4. Processing Fees & Timeline
- Registration within 21 days: Free of charge.
- Certified copy issuance fee: Rs. 50 per additional physical copy.
- Standard processing SLA: 7 working days from date of document verification.
- Reference number format: `APP-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`.

## 5. Grievance Redressal
If corrections in spelling or date are required, submit a Grievance citing the department code `CIV-REG`.
